Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue at github.com/skumbhat/bestys-site/issues. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

The assistant says it isn't available. The built-in assistant needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it, add your own model in bestys under Settings → Brain, or use bestys Lite (News, Notes and Games).

How do I connect my calendar or email? Open bestys, tap your picture at the top right, then Connections.

How do I delete my data? In bestys, tap your picture → Account → Delete everything. Everything bestys stored is erased from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.

How do I see or delete what bestys remembers? Tap your picture → Memory.
