Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Applies to the bestys iPhone, Apple Watch and widget apps

In short

bestys keeps your data on your iPhone. We, the developer, don't run servers that receive your content, don't have accounts for you, don't sell or share data for advertising, and don't track you. Data leaves your iPhone only to services you turn on, and bestys tells you before it does.

What stays on your iPhone

Your chats, notes, memories, activity log, settings and anything bestys reads from your calendar, reminders, contacts, Health, email or files are stored in an encrypted database on your iPhone. Its key is protected by the Secure Enclave, and the database is excluded from device backups. Apple Intelligence, the default assistant, runs on your iPhone (or in Apple's Private Cloud Compute, as Apple describes).

  • Health (sleep, steps, resting heart rate, workouts): read only, used only by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, never sent to any other model or service, never used for advertising, and never written.
  • Contacts: checked on your iPhone for birthdays and anniversaries.
  • Location: your approximate location, used to get the weather.
  • Microphone and speech: dictation is transcribed on your iPhone; audio isn't sent anywhere.
  • Memory: bestys asks before remembering a fact about you. You can see, edit and delete memories in Settings → Memory.

What we never do

  • Sell your data or use it for advertising or tracking.
  • Use your data to train AI models.
  • Send email, add events or reminders without your tap on an approval card (except a daily brief to yourself, if you turn that on).

Your choices

Turn any connection off in Settings → Connections. Delete everything bestys stored with Settings → Account → Delete everything, or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, you can also remove bestys from your Apple ID in iOS Settings.

Children

bestys isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page when bestys changes how it handles data, and note it in the app's What's new. Questions: bestys support.
